🌙 1. What is the Chinese Lunar Calendar?

The Chinese calendar is lunisolar based on moon phases and sun cycles. A lunar month has 29 or 30 days, making the lunar year ~11 days shorter than the Gregorian calendar.

📅 2. Why does the Gregorian date shift every year?

Because of the 11-day difference, a fixed lunar date shifts by 10 to 20 days on the Gregorian calendar each year. Standard recurring events in Google Calendar fail, so this app projects exact dates!

🔄 3. What is a "Leap Month" (闰月)?

To keep lunar months aligned with climate seasons, a 13th month is inserted ~7 times every 19 years. People born in a leap month celebrate on the regular month in standard years.

🐉 4. Chinese Zodiac Animals (生肖)

The Chinese Zodiac is a 12-year animal cycle: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, and Pig. Each year is represented by one sacred animal!
